# GLOBAL RELIEF ELPIS

## A Decentralized Global Relief & No-Food-Waste Network

## Vision and Mission

### Vision: No Food Waste, No Family Abandoned

The vision of Global Relief ELPIS is a world in which no edible food is wasted and no family is left alone when disaster strikes. In this world, surplus food flows naturally from those who have “too much” to those who have “too little,” and digital infrastructures ensure that any person affected by shock—whether a flood, war, fire or economic collapse—can access stable, transparent support quickly and with dignity.

### Mission: A Global Relief and No-Food-Waste Network

The mission is to develop and maintain a decentralized network that:

- Connects households, community cooks and donors with local buyers and beneficiaries.  
- Uses blockchain, AI, IoT and drones to coordinate safe, fast, verifiable exchanges of food and funds.  
- Incentivizes positive behavior with FAI, a token tied to nourishment and contribution.  
- Delivers stable, predictable disaster assistance using the ELPIS stablecoin.  
- Preserves privacy while keeping flows of value auditable and accountable.  
- Empowers communities, NGOs, churches and governments to collaborate on a shared humanitarian infrastructure.
